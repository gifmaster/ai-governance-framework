# Changelog

## Unreleased

- New page: agent-level controls, mapping agent controls (registry, permissions, approval points, memory and retention, logging, stop switch, vendors, usage and cost limits) to the AI Act, GDPR, NIS2 and DORA, marking what is law and what is practice

## 1.1 (October 2026)

Connects the framework to the tools and to the other laws that meet AI.

- Regulatory map: NIS2 (Danish implementation) and DORA, a table of where the laws overlap, and an overview of incident reporting deadlines under GDPR, NIS2, DORA and the AI Act
- Danish authorities: Styrelsen for Samfundssikkerhed (NIS2) and Finanstilsynet (DORA) added
- Incident report template: NIS2 and DORA notification checks, GDPR Art. 34
- Lifecycle, 90-day plan, oversight scale and templates now point to the tool for each step: EU AI Act Risk Check, AI Contract Clause Checker (with DORA pack), AI Use Case Register and Regulation Navigator
- README: toolkit descriptions updated, Danish summary extended

## 1.0 (October 2026)

First public version.

- Framework: principles, five dimensions, AI Oversight Scale with governance intensity matrix, use case lifecycle, roles and RACI, regulatory map
- Agile playbook for Scrum and SAFe
- Templates: AI policy, AI Council charter, use case intake, AI register, AI system record, incident report, backlog items
- 90-day getting started plan
- Aligned with the EU AI Act as amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)
